# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

EWAF adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Participation in this repository is subject to that code, including its standards, scope and enforcement guidelines. Be respectful, accept constructive feedback and do not harass or disclose another person's private information.

Reports should be sent privately to Thomas Lothian through the [GitHub repository owner profile](https://github.com/tlolabs) while a dedicated conduct email is **TBD**. Do not include sensitive details in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly, with reporter privacy respected. Thomas Lothian is the current enforcement contact and may remove harmful content or restrict participation under the Contributor Covenant guidelines.

Contributor Covenant 2.1 is by the Organization for Ethical Source and is available under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
